Before anything else

Two blockers to clear first

Neither is a creative problem. Both will stop a campaign dead.

No ads account is connected

Only GitHub is connected to this session, so I can't launch campaigns or spend budget. This kit is built to be executed by you or your media buyer — every step is spelled out. Connect an ads account and I can take it further.

No privacy policy page = automatic rejection

Meta and TikTok both require a reachable privacy policy URL at ad review. The app has no /privacy route and nothing in public/. Every campaign here gets rejected until that exists. It's a 20-minute fix — ask me and I'll build /privacy and /terms covering phone, email, Apple ID and uploaded video.

Web app, not a store app

There's no App Store or Play listing, so app-install campaigns aren't available. Everything below is website traffic / conversion, pointing at coolerup.lovable.app.

Strategy

Don't out-TikTok TikTok

You can't win on content volume. You win on positioning: TikTok is where people perform, coolerup is where they post the thing they'd normally delete. So the ads must never look like TikTok ads — no dancing, no trending audio, no polish. The moment the ad looks like a TikTok ad, the positioning collapses. Every ad should make someone think: that's the video I didn't post.

Impression→ Landing view→ Sign-up→ First watch→ First upload

Optimise for CompleteRegistration, never for clicks. Clicks are cheap and meaningless; you want people who finish the phone or Apple flow. Fire the event inside handleSuccess() in PhoneAuthDialog.tsx and onSuccess in EmailAuthForm.tsx — those are the exact hook points.

Execution

Launch sequence

Ten days from zero to scaling. Don't skip day 0.

  • Day 0 — publish /privacy and /terms · create ad accounts · add payment methods
  • Day 1 — install Meta, TikTok and Google pixels · verify CompleteRegistration fires on phone, email and Apple
  • Day 2 — build campaigns: 12 TikTok ads, 6 Meta ads, 2 YouTube ad groups
  • Day 3 — go live at 50% budget · touch nothing for 72 hours
  • Day 6 — first read · kill losers · double winners
  • Day 10 — full budget · retargeting live · lookalike at 100+ events
  • Day 30 — cut worst platform · concentrate spend · commission 3 new hooks
